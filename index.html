If Korea’s international students were a class of 100…

What comes next?

Korea passed its 300,000-student target a year ahead of schedule. The question is no longer how many students will come, but whether the country can educate and retain them.

Sources: Ministry of Education and KEDI, international student statistics for 2025 and 2026 (provincial, degree-level and gender tables), 2025 statistics by country, university and funding type, and the historical series 1999–2024. Nationality figures for 2026 and the September 2026 policy shift come from press reports of the Ministry’s release (Korea Herald, Seoulz). The nationality chart starts in 2018 because earlier breakdowns are not in the files; “Others” means everyone except China, Vietnam and Uzbekistan, and includes Mongolia in every year because 2026 is not split further. Province boundaries come from Statistics Korea (KOSTAT, 2018) and are simplified for display. Dots are rounded to 100 and are a visual simplification; 2018 and 2025 nationality shares are from the KEDI workbook.
